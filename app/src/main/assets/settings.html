APP SCANNER & FIREWALL



  
The scanner works fully offline: it looks at what each app is allowed to do, where it came from and whether it controls your phone. It is a safety check, not a virus-signature database (that would mean sending your app list to a company). Say "scan my phone" any time.

FLOAT & TYPER



  
  
Float mode talks to Pragon Live exactly like Pragon on the PC: one continuous voice conversation, you can interrupt it, and it calls the phone tools itself. Needs the Pragon (online) engine, an API key and Privacy Shield off (or generativelanguage.googleapis.com under Trusted hosts). If it can't connect, float mode falls back to the normal on-phone speech recognizer and voice.

  
  
Keeps the microphone open while Pragon speaks. Works best with headphones; turn it off if Pragon hears and answers itself on the loudspeaker.

  
  
While a video or music is playing, the float bubble stops keeping the microphone open (Google's speech recognizer takes the audio from other apps every few seconds, which makes YouTube stutter, pause and resume). The bubble dims; tap it once to say a command, or just pause the video. Turn this off to listen all the time.

  
  
In typer mode, if no text box has the cursor, Pragon picks the search / message / input box on the screen (never a password box) and types into it.

CALLS, AIRPODS & PRAGON PA



  Where Pragon's voice comes out
  

  

  
  
Connect AirPods or any Bluetooth headset and Pragon's voice and microphone go there. No headset: the speaker. Say "use the speaker", "use the earpiece", "turn off the headset mic". Android 12+ asks for the Bluetooth permission once.

  

  
  

  
When ON, "call Ravi" only dials names saved here (or a number you say, like "call 98765 43210"). Codes with * or # only open the dialer. Say "add Ravi 98xxxxxxxx to Pragon contacts"; leave the number out and Pragon copies it from your phone contacts. When OFF, names are looked up in your phone contacts instead and this list is ignored (it is kept, not deleted).

  

  
  

  

  
  
When a call comes in, Pragon says who it is and asks. Say OK to attend, ignore to leave it ringing, reject to cut it, or use the notification buttons. If you attend, Pragon answers on the loudspeaker, asks who, why, how urgent and a callback, never gives personal info, OTP or bank details, and flags scams. After the call you get a spoken summary and a notification; the last 20 are kept. Take the call back with the TAKE OVER button, one tap on the bubble, or "Pragon takeover". Android can't record the caller's side of a call, so the caller is heard through the loudspeaker; a few phones silence the microphone during calls.
